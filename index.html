{05} — About

The person behind the systems

I didn't take the traditional path. My education is rooted in scientific research, with a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. in Human Anatomy from Lead City University. That's where I learned to see operations as systems: patterns, precision, and processes that perform under pressure.

That same discipline now shapes how I support global teams, streamline operations, create meaningful customer experiences, and design brands with clarity, purpose, and a human touch. It carried me through frontline support and administrative operations at CVS Pharmacy and Sagility Health, and it still runs through everything I do.

Whether you're looking for a customer support specialist, a virtual assistant, or a creative partner to bring structure and thoughtful execution to your ideas, I'd love to work with you.

{10} — FAQ

Got questions?

01/Do you work across time zones?

Yes, I work fully remote with clients worldwide. I overlap comfortably with US and EU business hours and communicate async-first the rest of the time.

02/What are your core services?

Five packages: Customer Experience & Support, AI Prompt Engineering & Content Automation, Data Analytics & Business Intelligence, Digital Design & Content Creation, and Virtual Assistant Services. See the Packages & Rates section for what each one includes.

03/What do you charge?

Every package has a published starting rate, quoted in both USD and NGN, in the Packages & Rates section. Those are starting points: the final figure depends on scope, workload, and business needs, and custom packages are always available.

04/What tools do you use daily?

Zendesk and CRM platforms for support; Claude, ChatGPT, Jasper, and Midjourney for AI brand work; Canva and Photoshop for design; SQL, Power BI, and Excel for data; Notion, MS Teams, Buffer, and Later for operations.

05/How do we start working together?

Four steps: a discovery call, an audit and proposal, build-and-iterate with weekly check-ins, then handover with full documentation. See the process section above.

06/Can I see samples of your work?

Yes. The Selected Work gallery above shows samples by category, all viewable right here on the site. More available on request.

07/How quickly do you respond?

Within 24 hours, usually much faster.

08/Which industries have you worked in?

Healthcare and pharmacy retail, education, and tech and e-commerce startups.

09/Are you open to full-time roles?

Yes, I'm open to full-time remote roles as well as contract and project engagements.
