A Bit About Me

My approach is simple: precision over noise.

Systems, research, and data structures.

My academic background in scientific research trained me to view business operations as interconnected systems: where every detail, database entry, and customer touchpoint functions in absolute harmony.

B.Sc. Anatomy
M.Sc. Human Anatomy

From analyzing systems to managing operations.

I didn't take the traditional path to creative operations. My training is in advanced scientific research and systems analysis. Developing a rigorous, detail-oriented analytical mindset taught me to observe patterns others miss, query data points with absolute precision, and structure business workflows so they run seamlessly.

That methodical approach guided me through client-facing support and administrative operations at CVS Pharmacy and Sagility Health. There, managing database records, transactions, and direct customer interactions showed me that behind every data point is a real person. That is where I learned the value of clear communication, empathetic customer service, and organized administrative support.

Today, I combine that scientific background with modern creative tools. Whether I am querying customer feedback to guide brand strategy, automating content workflows with generative AI, designing clean social assets, or editing video reels, my goal remains the same: to build organized, data-driven systems that connect with people on a human level.
